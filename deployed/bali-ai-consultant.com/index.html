FAQ

Clear answers before the first call.

What does Bali AI Consultant do?

Bali AI Consultant builds practical AI systems for Bali businesses: AI training, WhatsApp automation, websites and custom AI-powered business operating systems for staff, rosters, SOPs, daily operations and dashboards. It is a Lumindra company founded by Mark Tulloch.

How much does it cost?

Prices are starting estimates in Indonesian Rupiah. AI Starter training is Rp 2,000,000, WhatsApp automation and websites start at Rp 5,000,000, and business operating systems start at Rp 10,000,000. Final scope is confirmed after discovery.

How long does a build take?

A Foundation operating system typically launches in 2 to 3 weeks. Operations Pro takes 3 to 5 weeks and Connected Intelligence 5 to 8 weeks.

Is this a replacement POS?

No. It can connect to POS data where vendor access allows, but it is not a replacement POS unless specifically scoped and integrated.

Do we need perfect data before starting?

No. We can start with the essentials, but dashboards, AI summaries, and integrations depend on the quality of available data.

Can WhatsApp automation be included?

Yes, where it is commercially useful. Official WhatsApp API/provider costs and message fees are separate unless included in the final scope.

Who owns the system?

The client owns its business data, brand assets, and supplied content. Reusable platform architecture, templates, methods, and core code remain with Bali AI Consultant unless otherwise quoted.
