Our Story

Born for Community & Cold Beer

What started as a humble neighbourhood bar has grown into a proper gathering place — where locals, expats, and curious travellers come together over real food, cold drinks, and honest conversation.

We're rooted in Aussie pub culture with a deep respect for Bali's soulful pace. The coffee's strong, the steak's sizzling, and the smiles are real.

This isn't fast service. It's real service.

Questions, Answered

Hours, happy hour, bookings and more — straight answers.

Where is Sun Shooters Bar & Grill?

Sun Shooters Bar & Grill is at Jl. Bidadari No. 66, Kerobokan, Kelod Kuta, Badung, Bali 80361, on Jl. Bidadari in the Kerobokan area.

What are the opening hours?

Sun Shooters is open every day of the week from 7:30am to 10:00pm.

When is happy hour and what are the deals?

Happy hour runs 3pm to 7pm, seven days a week. Cocktails and shots are 2-for-1, and a bucket of 4 Bintangs with your choice of sharing platter is Rp 210,000.

Do you serve breakfast?

Yes. Breakfast is served from 7:30am. The Big Breakfast is Rp 110,000, Eggs Benedict is Rp 105,000 and Avocado on Toast is Rp 95,000. Smoothie bowls are Rp 95,000 and Sunrise Smoothies are Rp 65,000.

How do I reserve a table?

Send a WhatsApp message to +62 821 4409 3838. There is no online booking system, so a WhatsApp message is all it takes.

Are menu prices inclusive of tax and service?

Yes. Menu prices include tax and service, so the price you see is the price you pay. Prices on the menu are shown in thousands of rupiah, so Rp 110 means Rp 110,000.

Can I order delivery?

Yes. Sun Shooters is available on GoFood and GrabFood.

What is on each night of the week?

Sunday is Sunday Roast & Rumbles, Monday is Monday Mashup, Tuesday is Tacos, Tequila & Tarot, Wednesday is Wings & Wheels, Thursday is Thirsty Thursday, Friday is Friday Night Lights with Reno live from 8pm, and Saturday is Global Sports Saturday.

Is Sun Shooters family friendly, and are there vegetarian options?

Yes. Tourists, families, expats and locals are all welcome. Vegetarian choices include the London Vegetarian Garden and Melbourne Vegetarian Pumpkin pizzas, vegetable nasi goreng and mie goreng, and Greek and Pumpkin salads.

What is the Sun Shooters Villa Partner Program?

It is a free program for villas around Kerobokan and Seminyak. Partner villas get printed menus, a QR card for the welcome book, guest perks on arrival and help with group bookings. Villa owners can join by WhatsApp on +62 821 4409 3838.
