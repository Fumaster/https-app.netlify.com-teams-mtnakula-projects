+ THE 30-DAY RESET PLAN

Four weeks. One focus each.

Delivered with your map

Five non-negotiable core rules for all thirty days, then a single focus per week — each with a daily add-on, tasks, a micro-challenge and a target outcome.

  1. Stabilise the system — body first, nervous system down, routine back
  2. Awareness + interrupt patterns — catch yourself in real time
  3. Rebuild self-trust — stop abandoning yourself
  4. Rewire relationships + identity — change how you relate, not just who you choose

If a rule breaks, you reset the next day. No spiral.

For parents · The Captain’s Map

The label is not the limit.

When a child gets a diagnosis — ADHD, ASD, twice-exceptional, “difficult” — the system hands you a category. What it rarely hands you is a map. High-output, fast-processing minds are managed as problems to be contained rather than systems to be understood, and the child learns to see themselves through what’s wrong with them instead of what’s remarkable about how they’re wired.

The old story

Diagnosis as identity

“Your child has X. Here are the medications, the accommodations, the coping strategies. Manage the symptoms.”

◆ The Captain’s Map

Architecture as identity

“Your child processes the world differently. Here is how their system actually works — and how to build on that.”

“A 13-year-old doesn’t need more labels. They need a Personal Operating System — and a parent who understands how to read it.”

The work

What is a Personal Operating System?

Not a therapy session. Not a report card. A structured, one-session deep dive that produces three concrete tools — for the child, and for you.

The Map

The Operating Map

A custom visual diagram of your child’s cognitive and emotional architecture. How they receive information, where they shut down, what unlocks them. Designed to be shown to them.

The System

The System Profile

A written reframe of their diagnosed traits as system features — not bugs. Their top three operating strengths and their two highest-risk pressure points.

The Script

The Parental Leadership Script

A practical communication framework built for how their mind works. What to say. What not to say. How to re-enter after conflict. How to build trust with their system.

Who this is for · parents of children 13+

You don’t need to tick every box. If one of these resonates, this session was built for you.

What is Conscious Systems?

Conscious Systems is Mark Tulloch's clarity practice in Kerobokan, Bali, and part of Lumindra. It offers Clarity Mapping, a 5-Session Journey through Awareness, Intention, Action, Reflection and Presence, Strategic Soul Consulting, and The Captain's Map for parents.

How much does a Clarity Mapping Session cost?

A Clarity Mapping Session costs IDR 750k to 1.5jt. The 5-Session Journey is IDR 5jt to 8jt, and Strategic Soul Consulting starts at IDR 10jt. Prices are in Indonesian Rupiah and confirmed on WhatsApp before anything is booked.

What do I get from a Clarity Mapping Session?

You get a seven-part Clarity Map, written by Mark in plain language and sent after the session: Situation, Story, Emotions, Pattern, Choice, Path Forward and Final Truth. It comes with a 30-Day Reset Plan you can start the same day.

Are sessions in person or online?

Both. Sessions run in person in Bali or online, one-to-one or in a small group. The 5-Session Journey runs weekly or fortnightly, at a pace set together.

What is The Captain's Map?

The Captain's Map is a one-hour session for parents of children aged 13 and over who have been labelled, for example with ADHD or ASD. The Identity Anchor session costs IDR 5,000,000 and produces an Operating Map, a System Profile and a Parental Leadership Script, with 14-day WhatsApp follow-up. It is not therapy, psychological assessment or a replacement for medical advice.

Who is Mark Tulloch?

Mark Tulloch spent more than three decades running high-pressure hospitality across the UK, Australia and Bali. He is a trained crisis counsellor, mental-health advocate and public speaker, the author of Bipolar Aware and The Pub, The Punch & The Path, and the founder of Lumindra in Bali.
